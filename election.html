The 2026 Shakopee City Council Election

On November 3, 2026, Shakopee votes for city council and mayor. Two at-large council seats are on the ballot, each a four-year term starting January 1, 2027, and every Shakopee voter can vote for two candidates — the top two vote-getters citywide win. Here is who is running for Shakopee City Council, the key dates, and how to cast your ballot.

Key Dates & How to Vote

Every Shakopee address is assigned an Election Day polling place. Enter your address in the State of Minnesota's official lookup and it will tell you exactly where you vote.

Find My Polling Place Official State of Minnesota lookup

Election Day

Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Polls are open 7 am – 8 pm.

Vote Early
  • Early voting: October 16 – November 2 Shakopee's location is City Hall (485 Gorman St)
  • Absentee voting starts September 18 Scott County Public Works (600 Country Trail E, Jordan)

Hours vary, so call Scott County Elections at (952) 496-8560.

Register to Vote

Minnesota lets you register right at your polling place on Election Day. Bring a Minnesota driver's license, state ID, or Tribal ID with your current address — or any photo ID, even expired or from another state, plus a current bill, bank statement, or lease.
