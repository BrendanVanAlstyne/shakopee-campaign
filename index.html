Share Your Story

If ICE has touched your life, your family, or your workplace here in Shakopee, your story matters — and you decide how it gets told.

Call or Text (763) 200-3711 Email brendanvanalstyne@gmail.com

What happens when you reach out

  1. You call, text, or email us — whatever's easiest. We'll set up a time to talk that works for you.
  2. We listen. In person, by phone, however you're comfortable. You tell us as much or as little as you want.
  3. You choose how it's shared: your own voice, a voice-altered recording, or written words with your name withheld. Nothing is decided for you.
  4. You review the final story — audio and transcript — before it ever goes on this site. You can change your mind at any point, for any reason.

We will never publish anything without your explicit, reviewed consent.

Vote Early
  • Early voting: October 16 – November 2 Shakopee's location is City Hall (485 Gorman St)
  • Absentee voting starts September 18 Scott County Public Works (600 Country Trail E, Jordan)

Hours vary, so call Scott County Elections at (952) 496-8560.

Not Registered? No Problem.

Minnesota lets you register right at your polling place on Election Day. Bring a Minnesota driver's license, state ID, or Tribal ID with your current address — or any photo ID, even expired or from another state, plus a current bill, bank statement, or lease.
