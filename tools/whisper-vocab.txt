This is an interview with Brendan Van Alstyne, a candidate for Shakopee City
Council in Shakopee, Minnesota, running alongside Ali Verney, with Angelica
Contreras for mayor. The interviewer is Tim Zunker of the Shakopee Chamber and
Visitors Bureau. Topics include the city property tax levy, Flock cameras, ICE
and the Metro Surge, Scott County, the Teamsters union, Parks and Rec, Canterbury
Park, Canterbury Commons, Valley Fair, Mystic Lake, the Renaissance Festival,
Rhythm on the Rails, Southbridge, Stillwater, Duluth, the Mall of America,
microgeneration, and the campaign slogan Strength in Community.
